# Campaign style guide (AI-analyse)

## Lighting
* **Source & Quality:** Bright daylight with a mix of direct overhead sunlight and soft, window/sky-diffused fill light.
* **Direction:** High top-down overhead, direct front-side sunlight, or side window lighting creating crisp highlights without blowing out detail.
* **Color Temperature:** Consistently warm to warm-neutral, ranging from 4800K to 5800K (averaging ~5400K daylight).

## Color palette

## Composition & framing
* **Camera Angle:** Dynamic off-axis perspectives—predominantly distinct low-angle tilt shots looking upward, or steep high-angle/bird's-eye close-ups.
* **Framing:** Intimate, tight close-ups and medium shots with strong diagonal axis orientation and subject offsets.
* **Negative Space:** Frequent use of open sky or dense natural foliage as clean background negative space.
* **Foreground Layering:** Heavy use of immediate foreground elements (hands, foliage, objects) framing the lens to establish depth.

## Lens & depth of field
* **Focal Length Impression:** 24mm to 35mm wide-angle perspective distortion with close camera proximity.
* **Depth of Field:** Shallow to moderate depth of field. Sharp focus on midground facial features paired with heavy, blurred foreground bokeh and soft background falloff.

## Grading
* **Contrast & Exposure:** High-key exposure with punchy contrast, crisp highlights, and open, detailed shadows.
* **Color Saturation:** Highly saturated, vivid primaries (greens, blues, pinks, oranges) paired with warm golden undertones.
* **Texture:** Clean digital clarity with subtle, filmlike highlight roll-off.

## Mood
Vibrant, optimistic, joyful, candid, energetic, intimate, and spontaneously authentic.

## Do's
* Simulate a 24mm–35mm wide-angle lens with subtle edge distortion and tight subject proximity.
* Position the camera at pronounced low angles looking upward or high angles looking downward.
* Frame shots with soft, out-of-focus foreground elements (e.g., fingers, leaves) partially obscuring the edge of the frame.
* Set daylight color balance between 5200K and 5800K for a consistent golden sun cast.
* Saturate primary colors (blues, greens, pinks) heavily while preserving realistic warm skin tones (`#E09C7B`).
* Use open sky or uniform foliage as dynamic, uncluttered background fill.

## Don'ts
* Do not use telephoto lenses (e.g., 85mm+) or compressed flat perspectives.
* Do not shoot directly at eye level with static, perfectly symmetrical framing.
* Do not apply cool cyan, desaturated, moody, or muted color grading filters.
* Do not use harsh, unlit studio black backdrops or dark crushed shadows.
* Do not clip highlights or generate flat, overcast grey skies.
* Do not render distant, detached wide-angle landscape framing without an intimate close foreground subject.
